A worldwide starting point

Materials, with a clearer
idea of the cost.

Choose a country and material to explore an indicative planning range. Local prices vary by supplier, grade, transport, tax and availability.

Important: these are broad, illustrative early-planning ranges—not live supplier quotes or verified national price lists for every country. Country selection applies a broad market-level adjustment, not a measured local index. Prices are materials-only, exclude labour, delivery, tax and installation, and can differ substantially within a country. Check local merchants and professionals before budgeting or buying.

Methodology context: RLB Construction Cost Indicator (national-currency indicative construction costs, Q2 2026) · JLL Global Construction Costs Index 2026 (global market variation and cost pressures).
